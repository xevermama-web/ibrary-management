Reading with curiosity

A library creates room for questions. Begin with one idea, connect it to an experience, and write down the question that follows. Learning grows through this small, repeatable practice.

A three-step reading practice

  1. Preview the structure and choose a question.
  2. Read actively and collect useful connections.
  3. Summarize the idea in your own words.

Discuss your notes with a friend, return to a difficult passage, and look for a second perspective. Curiosity is a practice you can carry beyond the library.
